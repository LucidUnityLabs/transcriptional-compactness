## Figure 1 — Patient-level random-effects meta-analysis forest plot

- **Source:** `exp/META_patient_level/forest_plot.png` (single panel, as-is)
- **Data files:** `exp/META_patient_level/results.json`,
  `per_patient_delta.csv`, `forest_plot_data.csv`
- **Panels:** A: per-cohort and pooled forest plot (35 patients,
  6 cohorts).
- **Caption draft:** Patient is the unit of inference. Per-patient
  Cliff's δ (malignant vs cell-type-matched comparator, within patient)
  combined by DerSimonian-Laird random-effects meta-analysis across 35
  patients in 6 cohorts: pooled δ = +0.51 [95% CI +0.44, +0.59],
  z = 12.9, p = 7.5 × 10⁻³⁸, I² = 96.8% (patient-clustered bootstrap
  95% CI [+0.37, +0.67]). Per-cohort pooled δ: Tirosh melanoma +0.91,
  Puram HNSCC +0.66, Olalekan ovarian +0.42, Darmanis GBM +0.29, Peng
  PDAC +0.10 (n = 2, underpowered), Chen prostate −0.08 (fails
  within-patient). Li CRC excluded (no patient-of-origin labels).

- **Caption draft:** Malignant cells carry higher per-cell mean
  Ollivier-Ricci κ than cell-type-matched non-malignant comparators in

  cohorts; panel G contrasts the liquid-tumour flip.

## Figure 3 — Robustness: hyperparameter grid and confound controls

- **Source PNGs:**
  - A: `exp/E5_hparam_sweep/e5_hparam_heatmap.png`
  - B: `exp/T1_umi_confound/t1_umi_confound_summary.png`
  - C: `exp/T5_proliferating_immune/t5_distributions.png`
  - D: `exp/T6_random_hvg_control/t6_random_hvg_summary.png`
- **Caption draft:** (A) 72-cell (HVG × PCA × k × α) grid: same-direction
  effect in 72/72 cells, median Cliff's δ +0.535, range [+0.285, +0.779].
  (B) UMI-quintile stratification (Darmanis): 5/5 quintiles same
  direction, strongest in low-UMI quintiles (δ +0.471 in Q1) — the
  opposite of a depth artefact. (C) Proliferating vs resting immune:
  δ = +0.198, vs malignant-vs-proliferating δ = +0.373 (p = 1.5 × 10⁻¹²)
  — proliferation is a modest additive contributor. (D) 20 random
  2000-gene panels: δ = +0.673 [+0.613, +0.721] with HVG-2000
  (z = −1.13) inside the random distribution.

## Figure 5 — Annotation-label and batch-correction sensitivity

- **Source:** `exp/FIGGEN_fig5.png` (generated by `exp/FIGGEN_fig5.py`,
  plotting only, from `exp/LABEL_VALIDATION/results.json` (panel A) and
  `exp/BATCH_CORRECTION/results.json` (panel B)).
- **Panels:** A: Chen prostate and Olalekan ovarian δ under original /
  alt1_broad / alt2_cluster annotation schemes; B: original vs Harmony vs
  random-null vs UMI-only δ for Tirosh / Puram / Darmanis.
- **Note (panel A):** `LABEL_VALIDATION/results.json` stores point deltas
  with exact p-values but no CIs, so panel A encodes MWU significance
  (filled marker p < 0.05, open n.s.) and overlays the fixed-comparator
  decomposition rather than drawing CIs.
- **Caption draft:** (A) Ovarian robust under all three schemes
  (δ = +0.63, +0.61, +0.37); prostate robust in direction (δ = +0.17,
  +0.42, −0.005; fixed-comparator decomposition positive for all three
  schemes: +0.047, +0.210, +0.062); no scheme reverses sign. (B) Harmony
  (patient as batch) attenuates the pooled effect (Tirosh δ +0.740 →
  +0.213; Darmanis +0.338 → +0.037) and sign-flips Puram (+0.340 →
  −0.523); random-embedding (mean δ ≈ −0.012 to +0.012) and UMI-only
  (δ ≤ 0.123) nulls stay ≈ 0.
